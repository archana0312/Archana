How identification works
  • Drone or not: shape under zoom. Rotors or a straight wing with a prop mean drone. Flapping wings, head and beak mean bird. Large fuselage with nav lights means manned aircraft.
  • Friendly or hostile: shape cannot tell you. Use the beacon or IFF reply, registered link, blue marking and strobe, and a flight pattern that matches known friendly activity.
  • Quad or fixed-wing can be either side, so never decide on shape alone.
  • No beacon, no markings and a straight run at the asset: treat as hostile and escalate. Never escalate on a poor image alone.
  • Wait for a better picture only while time to the zone allows.

Decision checklist
  • Watch the contact for several seconds before labelling.
  • Never label from one frame; use the track trail.
  • When unsure, report as unclassified rather than guess.
  • Confirming a drone raises an alert and saves a snapshot.
